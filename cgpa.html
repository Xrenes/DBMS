CGPA Calculator

Calculate your CGPA using UGC Uniform Grading System

How to Use the CGPA Calculator

1 / 4
1

Add Your Courses

Click the "Add Course" button to add each course you've taken. You can add as many courses as needed for your semester or entire program.

2

Set Credits & Grade

For each course, select the credit hours (1-6) and the grade you received (A+ to F). The UGC grading table on the right shows grade points.

3

Choose Category & Type

Mark each course as Major or Minor, and Theory or Lab. This lets you see separate CGPAs for major and minor courses.

4

View Your CGPA

Your Total CGPA, Major CGPA, Minor CGPA, and Total Credits update automatically as you add or modify courses. Formula: CGPA = Σ(Credits × Grade Point) / Σ(Credits)
